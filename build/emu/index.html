witchbox1

The Witchbox toys of Playbox on the M-VAVE FM-1, running in the browser from the firmware's own code. The band starts stopped: press PLAY.

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls.

Off. Sound starts when you switch it on.

This is the firmware itself, not a recording: the same C code the FM-1 build uses, with the FM-1's screen, buttons and 44.1 kHz output simulated. It has not yet run on a real FM-1.

Start here

  • It plays by itself. PLAY stops it; PLAY again writes a new track.
  • PRESETS changes the piece: another beat, tempo, key and sound.
  • EDIT jumps to the next section. REC holds the one you are in.
  • KNOB 1 to 4 set the four values at the bottom of the screen.
  • A key takes its note out of the scale or puts it back. The lit keys are the scale.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys.
  • To hold a button with a mouse: Shift-click or right-click it, then click it again to let go. HOME held + SELECT sets the tempo.
  • Hold any button for a second and the screen says what it does.

Keyboard

  • Space PLAY, N EDIT (next section), Enter REC (hold), Esc HOME.
  • ↑ ↓ pages, ← → pieces, Z X the vox lower and higher.
  • White keys: Q to I, then A to K. Black keys: 1 to 0 and -.

Pages and knobs

Night HOME
Tempo. Arc: how often the form moves on (0 stays in the Drop). Evolve: how often a pattern is rewritten. Dust: crackle and rain.
Drums SEQ
Level. Beat: 2-Step, Trap or March. Busy: how much is in the pattern. Swing.
Sub LFO
Level. Decay: a thud to a long 808. Drive. Glide.
Pad ENV
Level. Bright: the filter. Pump: how far the kick pushes everything down. Chord: Open, Triad, 7th, 9th.
Keys ARP
Level. Mode: Motif, Arp or Bells. Tone: Bell, Pluck, Saw. Octave.
Vox SEL
Level. Pitch: Screwed to Chipmunk. Chop: a choir at the bottom, stutters at the top. Vowel: dark to bright.
Space FX
Echo. Time: 1/8 to 3/8. Reverb. Warp: a tape that sags.
Setup GLO
Root. Chords: 1, 2 or 4 bars each. Tune. MIDI out. ALGORITHM sets the scale.

Saving

SAVE keeps your settings in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. Factory reset forgets them. The track itself is never kept: every PLAY writes a new one.
